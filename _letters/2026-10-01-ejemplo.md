# Example of the format: `published: false` keeps it out of the build. Copy it with a new name,
# write the letter and remove that line. `news` lists the _news items it brings (file name without
# the date) and, optionally, the letter's own lines for each; without `text`, the item's summary.
# The build writes /news/letters/<slug>/email.txt, the letter in Markdown to paste into the
# newsletter service.
title: "Carta de ejemplo"
date: 2026-10-01
published: false
news:
  - item: musadsl-1-0
    text: "Las líneas propias de la carta para esta noticia."
  - item: nota-gratuita
---
El texto de la carta, en Markdown. Los enlaces relativos (como [Works](/works)) se convierten en absolutos en el correo.
